Castles in the Sand · Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Castles in the Sand · Effective 2 September 2026 · Developer: Nate Voorhies

Castles in the Sand is a beach-building game for iOS, Android and the web. This policy explains what the game does with information about you. The short version: the game collects no personal data, and sends nothing anywhere.

What the game collects

Nothing. Castles in the Sand does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information, and has no way to. Specifically, the game:

  • has no account, sign-in or registration;
  • makes no network connections of any kind — it does not contact our servers (there are none) or anyone else's;
  • contains no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting and no social-media or third-party SDKs;
  • does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos or any other device sensor or data source;
  • does not use the advertising identifier or any other device identifier;
  • has no in-app purchases.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps two small settings files on your device, so it can remember your volume and quality settings and which levels you have finished. These files stay on your device, contain nothing about you as a person, and are removed when the game is uninstalled. You can also reset your progress from the game's Settings screen at any time.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and is not directed at any particular age group. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects no information from children.

The store you installed from

Apple's App Store and Google Play may collect information about app downloads, crashes and usage under their own privacy policies, which are separate from this one and which we do not control:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game ever changes what it does with your information — for example by adding an online feature — this policy will be updated first, the effective date above will change, and the change will be described here.
