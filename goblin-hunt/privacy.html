Goblin Hunt — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

Goblin Hunt is a single-player fantasy role-playing game published by Hexmonger. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information, on every platform it is played on: the browser version on itch.io, and the Android and iOS versions from the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

Questions, requests, or corrections: privacy@hexmonger.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no text entry of any kind. It never asks your name, your email, or anything else about you, and there is nowhere in it to type such a thing.
  • Your saved games stay on your device.
  • On phones and tablets the game shows ads, and the ad network — Google AdMob — collects information about the device in order to serve them. It is the only party the game contacts of its own accord, and on iOS and in the EEA/UK you are asked first.
  • If you ever choose to download an extra adventure, the computer you download it from sees your IP address, the way any web address you open does. Nothing is offered for download in the game as it ships, and no download ever happens unless you ask for one.
  • Anonymous gameplay diagnostics are described below. They are not enabled in the current release, and they will never carry anything that identifies you.
  • Buying "remove ads" ends the advertising data collection entirely.

1. Information the game does not collect

Goblin Hunt does not ask for, receive, or store:

  • names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses;
  • accounts, usernames, passwords, or social sign-ins;
  • contacts, calendars, photos, camera, or microphone (the game requests none of these permissions, on any platform);
  • precise location;
  • health, financial, or biometric information;
  • anything you type — the game contains no text field at all.

Character names in the game are generated by the game itself. They are not yours, and they are not treated as personal information.

Two exceptions are worth naming plainly, because they are the operating system's doing rather than the game's:

  • Android — the game participates in Android's automatic backup, so the save files listed above may be copied to your Google account's backup and restored onto a new phone. This is handled by Google under Google's privacy policy; the game cannot read that backup.
  • iOS — the game's files live in its Documents directory, which iCloud Backup and encrypted local backups include by default. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

You can delete everything the game has stored by deleting the game.

3. Advertising (Android and iOS only)

The mobile versions of Goblin Hunt are free and supported by ads. The browser version shows no ads and contains no advertising code at all.

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve, measure, and prevent fraud in advertising, Google's ad SDK collects and processes information from your device, which may include:

  • your device's advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID, or Apple's Identifier for Advertisers on iOS);
  • your IP address, and coarse location derived from it;
  • device and app information: model, operating system version, language, the app's identifier and version;
  • your interactions with the ads themselves — that an ad was shown, viewed, closed, or tapped.

Hexmonger does not receive this information. It goes to Google, which acts as an independent controller of it; Google's use is governed by Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Google's advertising partners are listed at Google's ad technology providers.

Your choice about personalised ads

  • In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the first time you open the game you are shown a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform, asking whether Google and its partners may use your data for personalised advertising. Nothing is requested from the ad network before you answer. Refusing is a complete answer: the game continues unchanged and shows non-personalised ads instead, which are chosen from the context rather than from anything about you.
  • On iOS, before any tracking can happen, iOS itself asks whether Goblin Hunt may track you across apps and websites. If you say no, the advertising identifier is not available to the game or to Google and ads are non-personalised.
  • You can change your mind at any time. In the game: menu → Settings → Privacy → Privacy & ad consent. On iOS you can also change it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • You can remove ads entirely with the one-off "remove ads" purchase in Settings. After that the ad SDK is never asked for an ad and stops collecting anything.

Payments

If you buy "remove ads", the purchase is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Hexmonger never sees your payment details, your name, or your billing address — only whether an anonymous purchase for this product succeeded.

4. Anonymous gameplay diagnostics

Goblin Hunt is a procedurally generated game, which means a bug often depends on the exact world it happened in. To fix such bugs and to understand which parts of the game people actually play, we intend to collect a small amount of anonymous gameplay information:

  • the world seed and the adventure module being played;
  • how a run ended — finished, abandoned, or died, and where;
  • progress markers: level reached, adventures completed, play time;
  • error and crash details from the game engine, and frame-rate measurements;
  • the game version, the platform, and the device model and OS version.

And, specifically, not:

  • any identifier of you or your device — no advertising identifier, no account, no email, no IP address stored alongside the records;
  • anything you typed, because there is nothing to type;
  • your location.

Records are grouped by a random identifier that is created on your device the first time the game runs, so that events from one play session can be read together. It is not derived from anything about you or your device, it is not shared with anyone, it is not combined with advertising data, and deleting the game destroys it. We do not attempt to re-identify anyone from this data, and we do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it to build a profile of you.

This is not enabled in the current release. No gameplay information leaves your device today. When it is enabled, this policy will be updated with a new effective date before the release that turns it on, and it will be possible to switch it off in Settings.

5. Downloadable adventures

Goblin Hunt can play adventures that did not ship inside it. There are two ways one can arrive, and only one of them touches the network:

  • From your own device. An adventure file you already have — dropped onto the game window, or left in the game's inbox folder — is read where it lies. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing is fetched.
  • From a catalogue. The game can also read a list of adventures kept at a web address and download one you pick from it. Downloading means asking the computer that hosts the file for it, and that computer necessarily sees your IP address, along with the ordinary details any web request carries — much what a browser reveals when you open a page. That host may keep its own server logs, under its own policy, which is not ours and which we do not control.

The game as it ships names no such address, so there is nothing to download and nothing is requested: the download screen says that no catalogue is set up, and the address has to be entered by hand before anything is asked of anyone. Only `https://` addresses are fetched.

If we offer downloadable adventures ourselves — paid or free — this policy will be updated with a new effective date before the release that turns them on, and the address they come from will be named here.

Whichever way an adventure arrives, it is only data the game reads: an adventure may contain text, maps, quests and images and nothing else. It cannot run code, ask for permissions, reach the network, or send anything of yours anywhere.

7. Children

Goblin Hunt is a fantasy adventure with combat and depictions of blood. It is not directed at children, and it is not made for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because of that, the game does not request child-directed ad treatment from the ad network; it does restrict ad content to a teen-appropriate rating. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this game, write to privacy@hexmonger.com and we will act on it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict its use, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent you previously gave. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already happened.

For the personalised advertising described in section 3, the legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time from Settings → Privacy. For non-personalised ads and for the diagnostics in section 4, the basis is our legitimate interest in funding and repairing the game — an interest we have deliberately kept narrow by not collecting identifiers at all.

Because the game holds no account and no identifier of you, we usually cannot find "your" data on request, and we will say so rather than guess. What we can always do is tell you exactly what a given version of the game collects, and act on any request about the advertising consent you gave. For the data Google holds as a controller, exercise your rights with Google directly through Google's privacy tools.

If you are in California: we do not sell your personal information for money. Serving personalised ads may count as "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising under the CPRA; you can opt out of it with the in-game privacy options, by declining tracking on iOS, or by buying "remove ads".

If you are in the EEA or the UK and are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to your national data protection authority.

Requests: privacy@hexmonger.com. We answer within 30 days.

9. The browser version

The version of Goblin Hunt playable in a browser is hosted on itch.io. It contains no ads, no ad SDK, and no diagnostics; its saves live in your browser's local storage and are cleared when you clear site data. The page itself is served by itch.io, whose own logging and cookies are governed by itch.io's privacy policy.
