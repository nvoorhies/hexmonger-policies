Common questions

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. Tin Coffins is a single-player game with no accounts, no online play and no server of ours to connect to. Steam itself may use the network to unlock achievements and sync saves, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Where are my saves?

In the game's own folder in your user account on your computer; they are not on a server. With Steam Cloud on, Steam keeps a copy with your Steam account and restores it on any computer you play on. You can turn Steam Cloud off in the game's Properties in Steam.
